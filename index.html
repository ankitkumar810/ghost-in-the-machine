Why Open-Source AI is Mandatory for This

🔒 Absolute Privacy for Sacred Memories

When building a digital ofrenda or transcribing remembrances of departed ancestors and loved ones, sending private grief, personal family photos, or intimate stories to a corporate cloud API is unacceptable. Open-weight models running on local silicon ensure that no server, advertiser, or remote log ever touches your sacred remembrances.

🕯️ Works When the Lights (and Internet) Are Off

Cloud AI dies when the Wi-Fi drops, when a credit card expires, or when an API provider changes its terms of service. An open-weight model running locally can operate in a mountain cabin, during a blackout, or 50 years into the future without paying rent to a corporation.

⚡ Uncensored Channeling of History

Corporate model guardrails frequently sanitize discussions of death, mortality, historic controversies, or obsolete software vulnerabilities. Open-source models grant creators the freedom to craft authentic, poetic, and historically faithful personas like Lovelace, Turing, or obsolete language spirits without sanitized corporate PR speak.

💸 Zero Marginal Cost

Zero API tokens, zero subscription paywalls. Once downloaded, local open models can converse with you forever for the cost of electricity alone.
