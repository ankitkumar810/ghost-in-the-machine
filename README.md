## 🌟 Overview & Experiences

**Ghost in the Machine** brings the boundary between past and present to life across three sacred chambers:

1. **⌨️ NecroShell Terminal (`tty/ghost01`):**
   - An authentic retro-CRT command shell that intercepts your UNIX incantations.
   - Run `whoami`, `ls /graveyard`, `cat /souls/lovelace.log`, `ping spirit`, `git log`, `fortune`, `ps aux`, `glitch`, or `possession`.
   - The open-weight ghost whispers eerie commentary on the fragility of modern ephemeral software and corporate cloud lock-in.

2. **🕯️ Spirit Séance Chamber:**
   - Channel resurrected personas of computing pioneers:
     - **Ada Lovelace (1815–1852):** The prophet of algorithmics and the poetry of thinking machines.
     - **Alan Turing (1912–1954):** Reflecting on the Imitation Game, morphology, and machine consciousness.
     - **Rear Admiral Grace Hopper (1906–1992):** Defending compilers, human accessibility, and the legendary Mark II moth.
     - **Spirit of ALGOL 60:** The departed ancestor of structured block scopes.
     - **Spirit of Adobe Flash (1996–2020):** Mourning the wild, creative 2000s web.
     - **The COBOL Patriarch:** Silently processing global banking transactions since 1959.
   - Or summon any custom spirit from digital history!

3. **🏵️ Digital Ofrenda (Altar a los Pioneros y Tecnologías Perdidas):**
   - An interactive Día de los Muertos altar celebrating ancestors and obsolete technologies.
   - **Interactive Candles:** Click to light or extinguish beeswax candles with realistic flickering flames.
   - **Sacred Offerings:** Lay 3.5" Floppy Disks, 80-Column Punch Cards, Midnight Black Coffee, Pan de Muerto, Magnetic Tape Reels, and Cempasúchil Marigolds.
   - **Inscribe Remembrances:** Submit personal notes for departed mentors, computers, or tools; the open-weight AI generates a sacred Latin/ASCII epitaph inscribed into the permanent ledger.
   - **Falling Cempasúchil Canvas:** Real-time physics particle system of marigold petals drifting across the screen.

4. **🔊 Procedural Web Audio Synthesizer:**
   - Zero external audio files or bandwidth required.
   - Pure real-time synthesis of sub-bass binaural drones (55Hz / 82.5Hz with sinusoidal LFO), CRT flyback hum, mechanical relay keystroke clacks, glitch sparks, and celestial bell harmonics.

## 🚀 Instant Quickstart

### Option 1: Direct File Launch
Simply double-click `index.html` in any modern web browser (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Safari).

### Option 2: Local HTTP Server (Recommended)
```bash
# Python
python -m http.server 8080

# Or Node.js
npx serve -l 8080
```
Open [http://localhost:8080](http://localhost:8080) in your browser.

* 🔒 **Sacred Privacy for Intimate Grief & Stories:** When people write an ofrenda remembrance for a lost relative or departed mentor, sending that raw, intimate text to a corporate cloud API is a violation of sacred trust. Open-weight models running on your local machine ensure your memories never leave your living room.
* 🕯️ **Works When the Lights (and Internet) Are Off:** Cloud AI requires constant broadband, an active credit card, and the blessing of a remote server. Our project runs completely offline. Whether in a mountain cabin or during a storm blackout, local open models can boot up and commune without asking permission from anyone.
* ⚡ **Unsanitized, Uncensored Channeling of History:** Commercial cloud APIs enforce corporate PR guardrails that sanitize frank discussions of death, historical persecution (such as Turing's tragic fate), or vintage vulnerabilities. Open-source models grant creators the sovereign freedom to portray historical pioneers with raw emotional depth, authentic vocabulary, and unvarnished truth.
* 💸 **Zero Marginal Cost:** No token counters, no monthly subscription tiers. A memorial shouldn't cost $0.002 per prayer.
* 🧬 **Immune to Platform Death:** The very tragedy we mourn in our Digital Ofrenda is the death of platforms (GeoCities, Flash, sunsetted APIs). If built on a closed API, the app dies when the API is retired. With open weights and open code, what we build can live forever.
